Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our tax, auditing, and business compliance advisory services.

What services does Sunny Balani & Co. provide?

Sunny Balani & Co. provides a comprehensive suite of professional services including statutory auditing, corporate tax planning, GST filing, F&O (Futures & Options) trading taxation, NRI taxation, startup registration u/s DPIIT, and business valuation.

Does the firm specialize in stock market, F&O, and equity trading taxation?

Yes, absolutely. Under the guidance of CA Sunny Balani, who is an having experience handling taxation and reporting issues involving equity, intraday and derivatives transactions, the firm specializes in F&O income computation, ITR-3 reporting and tax audit support where Section 44AB is applicable, capital gains tax planning, intraday equity compliance, and expert ITR-3 tax filing.

Where is the firm located and what geographic areas do you serve?

Our physical office is located in Narayan Peth, Pune, Maharashtra. We provide year-round direct tax, auditing, and corporate compliance services to businesses and professionals throughout Pune, Mumbai, Ahilyanagar, and virtually across India.
